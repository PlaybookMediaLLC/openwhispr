Oppulence Voice is the capture product in the Oppulence suite. Transcription and
local note management remain on the desktop. Cloud synchronization carries
encrypted artifacts, and Rowboat ingestion happens only after an explicit
user-controlled handoff.

## Product boundary

- **Oppulence Voice** captures, transcribes, organizes, and encrypts voice artifacts.
- **Rowboat** converts approved artifacts into durable relationship evidence and workflows.
- **The API** authenticates devices, relays encrypted sync items, and records consented handoffs.
